How to Play Shikaku

1
Divide into Rectangles

Partition the entire floorplan into non-overlapping rectangular or square tatami rooms.

2
Match Room Area to Clue

Every room must contain exactly one number clue, and the room's total area (Width × Height) must equal that number.

3
Complete the Floorplan

Every cell on the floor must belong to a room without any gaps or overlapping rooms.

Controls & Tips

  • Draw Room: Touch/click and drag from one corner to the opposite corner. Or tap one corner, then tap the opposite corner!
  • Remove Room: Tap an existing room to delete it.
  • Deductive Hints: Tap 💡 anytime for logical hints based on prime factoring and forced bounds.
